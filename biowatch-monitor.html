🤖 How the AI works (the honest version)

We trained a model in Google Teachable Machine on our own photos of water — clear, cloudy, and green. It runs on the device, and when a pond's camera takes a picture, the AI guesses which one it looks most like and gives a confidence for each. It also watches the last few readings and warns us when a pond is trending greener — so we catch a bloom early. It isn't magic: it only learned from the pictures we showed it, it can be wrong, and it only sees color — not poisons or germs. So when the AI flags a pond, a real person still checks and tests the water.

Reconnecting the Living Ground Beneath Us

Driving question: How can we reconnect a world that people have divided?
Do it live: pour water on the bare-soil tray. 💧
The problem

People divide the world

  • Roads and buildings cut into hills — and cut their roots
  • Clearing forests & mowing takes away the plants that hold soil
  • Wildfires burn the plants, so rain can't soak in
Say one fact you checked: e.g. after a wildfire, less rain soaks into the soil.
The science

The underground army

  • Grasses — a root net that holds the top layer
  • Deep-rooted natives — long anchor roots pin the soil
  • Fungi & worms — glue soil crumbs, help rain soak in
Show: the root-window cups — different plants, different roots, different jobs.
Our solution · Heal

The Root Team Mat

A seed blanket that rots away — grasses sprout first, deep roots anchor next, wildflowers bring back bees & butterflies, and it leaves a living hillside.

Show the mat.
Our solution · Watch

The RootWatch sensor

A LEGO stake with tilt + wetness sensors. If the soil soaks or the stake leans, it flashes and sounds an alarm — a warning before the slide.

What we found

Our storm test

  • Three trays, same soil, same angle, same rain — bare vs. lawn grass vs. our root team
  • We weighed the mud that washed off, 3 tries each
  • Result: the root-team tray lost the least soil — mixed roots beat bare ground and lawn alone
Show the results table. We kept the versions that failed — they show how we improved.
Our recommendations

What we'd tell a community

  • Cover bare slopes with a Root Team Mat — mixed deep + shallow roots, not just lawn
  • Stop mowing steep slopes so deep roots can grow
  • Put a RootWatch sensor above homes, roads & streams — and act on the alarm
  • Heal a bare or burned hillside before the next big storm
  • Plants help shallow slides; deep landslides still need engineers

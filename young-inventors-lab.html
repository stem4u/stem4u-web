Learn it, then make it

Every great invention starts with an idea or a problem worth solving. At Young Inventors Lab, students learn the fundamentals of design and engineering by working on hands-on challenges.

Students explore 3D design using Onshape, discover how everyday objects and mechanical systems work, and learn how to turn their ideas into physical creations using 3D printing.

Rather than simply following instructions, students develop their own designs, experiment with different approaches, test their creations, and make improvements along the way. Every project is an opportunity to think like an inventor and work like an engineer.

What students will explore

A learning journey from creative ideas to working prototypes.

Imagine & Design

Learn 3D CAD

Explore ideas and learn 3D CAD fundamentals by designing useful objects such as phone stands, keychains, and desk accessories.

Explore Mechanisms

How things move

Discover gears, linkages, moving parts, and how mechanical systems work.

Create & Prototype

Print your designs

Learn how 3D printers turn digital designs into physical objects, and explore materials, dimensions, and design constraints.

Build & Test

Make it work

Create prototypes, test their functionality, identify problems, and improve their designs.

Solve Challenges

Engineering challenges

Work through creative challenges such as marble runs, mechanical toys, and small functional devices.

Invent

Something new

Apply everything learned to design and present an original invention.

What students will be able to do

By the end of the program, students will be able to:

  • Create original 3D designs using Onshape CAD tools
  • Understand basic mechanical design concepts and how things work
  • Turn digital designs into physical prototypes using 3D printing
  • Apply engineering principles to solve practical design challenges
  • Test, evaluate, and improve their own creations
  • Explain and present their ideas with confidence

What students will create

From ideas to real creations.

  • Creative product designs — phone stands, desk accessories, and personalized creations
  • Mechanical inventions — gears, linkages, and moving mechanisms
  • Engineering challenges — marble runs, structures, and design challenges
  • Original inventions — student-designed projects and prototypes

Tools and resources

Everything students need to explore design and engineering is here every week:

  • Onshape 3D CAD software
  • Computers and design resources
  • 3D printers and prototyping materials
  • Engineering tools and project components
  • Instructor guidance and project feedback

Where this leads

Young Inventors Lab builds a foundation in engineering design, creative problem-solving, and manufacturing. Students who want to explore engineering further can continue into more advanced programs such as Engineering Design Studio, where they develop deeper skills in mechanical design, assemblies, prototyping, and manufacturing. For students interested in robotics, these skills also provide a useful foundation for exploring FTC robotics and other advanced engineering projects.

How the program works

STEM classes for Leesburg families

An easy trip south from Leesburg and Lansdowne — close enough for weeknight classes and Saturday builds.

For families in Loudoun's county seat

Leesburg blends historic community with fast-growing new neighborhoods, and its families are looking for enrichment that actually sticks. STEM4U gives Leesburg students a place to move from curiosity to capability — building and coding real robots, then branching into drones, CAD, or a FIRST competition team when they're ready.

What your student will do

  • Build working robots with motors, gears, and sensors
  • Learn to code — from block-based programming toward real Python and Java
  • Program and fly drones, and design parts in CAD
  • Solve hands-on engineering challenges with real mentors
  • Join a FIRST LEGO League or FIRST Tech Challenge team when ready

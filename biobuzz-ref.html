🌸 FLOWER Ownership — Key Mechanic
👑
Owner = alliance whose NECTAR is the top-most in the FLOWER
🎯
Owner scores +2 pts for every piece inside — POLLEN + both NECTARs + opponent's pieces. Max 8 pieces per FLOWER
⚔️
Placing your NECTAR on top of the opponent's transfers ownership — a key late-match move
⬇️
Bottom NECTAR Bonus (+5 per FLOWER) — alliance with NECTAR deepest in each FLOWER. Can be a different alliance than the owner. Max 20 pts across all 4 FLOWERs
💡
Max single FLOWER: 8 pieces ×2 = 16 + BNB 5 = 21 pts · All 4 FLOWERs dominated = 84 pts

♟️ Strategy Tips
🐝
HIVE TIPs win matches — 20 pts + unlocks NECTAR from ALLIANCE AREA
🌸
Own the FLOWER — place your NECTAR last on top to score all pieces inside
📈
SWARM RP is nearly free — Both LEAVE (6) + AUTO PARK ×2 (10) + TELEOP PARK ×2 (10) = 26 pts. Need only 16. Design for it every match
🎯
4 Tips for P1 RP, 7 for P2. Target 2 tips/robot/match
🌿
Clear CELLS — opponent scores 2 pts each for pieces left in upward-facing CELL
💎
Max FLOWER = 21 pts — own the top + bottom NECTAR with 6 POLLEN inside. Both can be the same alliance

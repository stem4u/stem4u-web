BioWatch

Protecting our living systems — land, water and air

Driving question: How can we protect the living things around us when their land, water and air are all under threat?
The big picture

Biodiversity is hit from three sides

  • 🌳 Land — forests cut down, habitat lost
  • 💧 Water — pollution harms fish, turtles, birds
  • 🌫 Air & people — dirty air, traffic, litter
Say one fact you checked.
Our big idea

Orca BioWatch

One system that watches a habitat's health and helps on all three fronts — so we keep every idea, not just one.

Land

Replant natives + little animal homes

Water

Red/yellow/green monitor + filter

Air & people

Walk/bike, trash robot, awareness

Start with one piece

We build the Water module first

We can't build it all at once — so we prototype the easiest-to-measure piece: the water monitor + filter.

Measure

Cloudiness (turbidity) via LED + light sensor → green/yellow/red

Filter

Removes suspended sediment

Alert

Flow drops → "needs cleaning"

What we found

Our filter test

  • Same dirty-water recipe (grams of soil per liter), 3 tries
  • Read cloudiness before & after, and check it's still flowing
  • Compare filter designs — keep the fails
Show the before/after chart and the LEGO pool + drain.
Why it matters & what we'd say

Clearer water = more life

  • Less sediment → more sunlight & oxygen → healthier fish, insects & plants
  • Give the caretaker a green/yellow/red action card (watch · rinse · replace)
  • Put it where sediment enters; share data with a watershed group
  • Honest: clearer isn't always safe — pair it with a real water test
  • Grow BioWatch to the land and air modules next

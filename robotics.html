Where it leads

Where every builder starts

Robotics is our foundational, year-round program — the on-ramp to everything else we do. Students learn to design, build, wire, and code real robots in a hands-on workshop, at their own pace and without the pressure of a competition season. It's where curiosity turns into capability.

Who it's for

Robotics is built for students in grades K–12, at any experience level. Complete beginners start with the fundamentals of building and block-based coding; returning students take on harder builds, sensors, and autonomous programming. No prior experience — and no team commitment — required.

What your student will do

  • Build working robots with motors, gears, and sensors
  • Learn to code — from block-based programming toward real text code
  • Solve hands-on engineering challenges and debug when things break
  • Work through projects at their own pace, guided by real mentors
  • Build the skills and confidence to join a competition team when ready

The foundation that leads somewhere

Unlike a one-off class, our Robotics program is the first rung of a real pathway. Students who start here are ready to step onto a competition team — FIRST LEGO League Challenge in elementary and middle school, then FIRST Tech Challenge in the higher grades. Start whenever you're ready and grow as far as you want to go.

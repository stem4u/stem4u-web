STEM classes for Aldie & Stone Ridge families

Home base. STEM4U is located in the Aldie / Dulles South area — the closest option for Aldie, Stone Ridge, and Willowsford families.

Right in your community

STEM4U is rooted in the Aldie and Stone Ridge community. For local families that means a real engineering workshop within minutes — where kids build and code robots, take on FIRST LEGO League and FIRST Tech Challenge, and grow along a K-12 pathway without a long commute.

What your student will do

  • Build working robots with motors, gears, and sensors
  • Learn to code — from block-based programming toward real Python and Java
  • Program and fly drones, and design parts in CAD
  • Solve hands-on engineering challenges with real mentors
  • Join a FIRST LEGO League or FIRST Tech Challenge team when ready

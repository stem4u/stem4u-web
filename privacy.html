Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your family's information — and how we keep our students' privacy at the center of everything we do.

Effective: August 5, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 5, 2026

STEM4U is preparing to open in Northern Virginia. This policy describes our current data practices and is being finalized ahead of enrollment. Questions? Email contact@stem4u.com — we're happy to walk you through it.

1. Who we are and what this covers

STEM4U ("STEM4U," "we," "us," or "our") is a STEM and robotics enrichment program serving families in Loudoun County and Northern Virginia. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

It applies to stem4u.com and all of its subdomains (including our learning site at learn.stem4u.com), to our online forms (such as our waitlist and contact forms), and to information we collect when you enroll a child or interact with us by email, phone, text, or in person. By using our sites or giving us information, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Families and children come first

STEM4U serves children, and protecting their privacy is central to how we operate. Two principles guide everything below:

  • The account belongs to the parent or guardian, not the child. Personal and billing information is held under the adult's account.
  • We collect as little about a child as possible. In our lab there is no child account and no child email address is ever collected. If we later offer an at-home learning website, a child signs in with a class code, first name, and PIN — still no email.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • When you join our waitlist or contact us: the parent's or guardian's name, email, and phone number; your child's first name, age or grade; and anything you write in a message.
  • When you enroll: the information above plus billing details to process payment (card data is handled by our payment processor — see Section 7 — and we do not store full card numbers), emergency contacts, and any allergy, accessibility, or medical notes you choose to share so we can keep your child safe.
  • Media consent: if you opt in, permission to photograph or record your child's work or participation.

Information about students

On the class roster we use a child's first name only (no email). If we offer an at-home learning site, a student signs in with a class code, first name, and PIN, and may save coding projects they create.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our analytics providers may collect basic technical information — such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring links — through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 8). We do not knowingly collect a child's precise location or any sensitive data beyond what a parent voluntarily provides for safety.

4. How we use information

  • respond to waitlist signups, inquiries, and coach requests;
  • enroll students, run classes and camps, and manage teams;
  • process payments and send receipts and account notices;
  • keep children safe (emergency contacts, allergy/medical notes, background-checked staff);
  • communicate with parents about schedules, progress, and program updates;
  • with your consent, send occasional STEM4U news (you can opt out anytime);
  • operate, secure, and improve our websites; and
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not sell your or your child's personal information, and we do not use children's information for behavioral advertising.

5. How we share information

  • Service providers who work on our behalf — our payment processor, class-registration/scheduling software, email provider, and website host — only as needed to provide their service to us, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Competition programs. If your child participates on a team (such as FIRST LEGO League or FIRST Tech Challenge), the registration information those programs require is shared, and their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.
  • Legal and safety reasons — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect the safety of children, staff, or the public, or in connection with a business transfer.

We never sell personal information, and we never share children's information for third-party marketing.

6. Children's privacy (COPPA)

We comply with the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and design our program to minimize children's data.

  • We do not create online accounts for children under 13 or ask them for an email address.
  • Where any child personal information is involved, we obtain verifiable parental consent at enrollment, and the parent controls that information.
  • We collect only what is reasonably necessary for a child to participate.
  • A parent or guardian may review their child's information, ask us to delete it, and refuse further collection by contacting us (Section 11). Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide certain parts of the program.
  • We keep children's information only as long as needed and then delete it.

7. Payments

Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor. We do not store your full payment card number on our systems. The processor's handling of your payment data is governed by its own privacy policy and by applicable card-industry (PCI) standards.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our sites may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, keep the site secure, and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect how the site works. We honor recognized browser opt-out signals where required by law.

9. How long we keep information, and how we protect it

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide our services, meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Waitlist information is kept until you ask us to remove it or it is no longer needed. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards — including access controls, staff background checks, and limiting who can see family data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what you entrust to us and to limit what we collect in the first place.

10. Your privacy rights

Under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA) and similar laws, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct inaccuracies, delete it, obtain a copy of information you provided, and opt out of targeted advertising or any "sale" of personal data (note: we do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising). To exercise any of these rights, contact us (Section 11); we will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. For children's information, a parent or guardian exercises these rights on the child's behalf.

11. SMS / text messaging

If you provide a phone number and opt in, we may text you about your child's participation, schedules, or program updates, and — only with your consent — occasional STEM4U news. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of enrolling. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing.

12. Links to other sites

Our sites may link to third-party websites (for example, FIRST, or a coding tool we recommend). We are not responsible for their privacy practices — please review their policies before providing information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes affecting children's information, seek parental consent where the law requires it. Your continued use of our sites after an update means you accept the revised policy.

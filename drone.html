What is the Drone program?

Drones are one of the most engaging ways into real engineering — and among the fastest-growing skills in the world, from filmmaking to agriculture to search-and-rescue. In our Drone program, students learn to fly safely, program autonomous flight paths, and understand the physics and engineering that keep an aircraft in the air. It's hands-on, it's real hardware, and it's a blast.

Who it's for

The Drone program is built for students in grades 5–12, at any experience level. Beginners start with the fundamentals of safe flight and controls; more experienced students move into autonomous programming, aerial photography, and building or repairing their own aircraft.

What your student will do

  • Learn to pilot real drones with confidence and control
  • Program autonomous flight paths and missions in code
  • Explore aerial photography, mapping, and first-person-view (FPV) flying
  • Understand the physics of flight and the engineering inside a drone
  • Practice safe, responsible flying and the basics of airspace rules
